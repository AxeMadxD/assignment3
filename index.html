Our work

What happens after the call

A bird that reaches us alive has about two hours of useful time. This is the route from the moment the phone rings to the moment the bird is a wild bird again, and it is the part of the page I built with my own media queries.

  1. 01

    Rescue and transport

    We ask the finder to put the bird in a dark box, with no food and no water, and to keep it warm and quiet. Our van covers the district in under two hours. Most calls come from shepherds and from the power company.

    Average: 11 calls a month

  2. 02

    Rehabilitation

    X-ray on the first day, then a fracture is pinned and the bird spends six to ten weeks in a quiet box aviary. The last stage is a forty metre flight corridor, where the bird has to hunt live prey before anybody talks about release.

    Average stay: 74 days

  3. 03

    Release and monitoring

    A bird goes back to the valley where it was found, ringed and weighed, on a clear morning with a full crop. Two of our eagles carry light transmitters, so pupils in Berel can follow their flights on the map in the classroom.

    66% of admitted birds fly again

Visit

Before you come to the centre

We are a hospital first and a visitor centre second, so the rules below are not decoration. Read them, and the birds will not pay for the visit.

Cover the bird with a jacket, put it in a cardboard box with air holes, close the box and keep it in a dark, quiet, warm place. Do not give food or water, and do not try to straighten a wing. Then call the rescue line and send us a photo of the place where you found it, because this is where the bird has to go back.

No. Patients in the hospital block and in the flight corridor never see visitors, because a bird that gets used to people cannot be released. On the tour you meet the nine permanent residents, who cannot go back to the wild and have been living with us for years.

Cameras and phones are welcome, flash is not. Owls are the most sensitive, so in the owl aviary we ask everybody to put the phone away completely. On the first Saturday of the month we run a photography morning with good light and no group noise.

Write to us two weeks before through the form below. School groups from the Katon-Karagay district pay nothing, and we bring the programme to the school if the road is closed by snow.
